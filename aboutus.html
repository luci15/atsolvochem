About Us

A. T. Solvochem, is one of the leading suppliers, traders and distributors of a wide range of Chemicals, Petrochemicals and Industrial Polymers. Owing to the high demands of our clients we offer a wide range of products such as Hydrocarbons, Alcohols, Glycol, Ketones, Esters, Chlorinated Solvents, Fragrance Chemicals, Monomers And Others.

With our professional approach and association with well recognized vendors, we have achieved overwhelming success in the market. Further, our organization is located in Mumbai with advanced facilities and regularly availability of stock. Before the products are delivered to clients, our quality controllers give lab analysis report with each dispatch, to assure complete quality. In addition, our products are packed with quality drums to protect material.

Under the able guidance of our Mentor, Mr. Parag Shah, we have established a strong position in the market. Our transparent business dealings has helped us in gaining a vast clientele in the domestic market. Furthermore, with the support of our efficient team members, our organization has achieved consistent growth in the respective domain.
