Who we are

Trusted by manufacturers across India.

A.T. Solvochem is a leading importer, distributor and supplier of chemicals, petrochemicals and industrial polymers. Under the leadership of Mr. Parag Shah, the firm has completed two decades supplying chemicals and solvents — beginning by trading solvents and chemicals in a single market.

Years of dedication and hard work have made us a trusted supplier. We value our customer relationships and our work ethic: we strengthen old bonds and create new ones. Active networking has built relationships with leading manufacturers across the country, so we can be a one-stop shop for every industry.

We offer hydrocarbons, alcohols, glycols, ketones, esters, chlorinated solvents, fragrance chemicals, monomers and more.
