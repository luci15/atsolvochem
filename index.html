Questions

Asked before you ask.

Still not answered? Ask the sales desk — a straight answer either way.

Ask a question
How do I place an order or get a quote?

Send us the chemical name, grade and quantity through the enquiry form, WhatsApp or phone. We confirm availability and pricing from current stock, or source it for you.

What packing do you offer?

Tanker, FCL, barrels and bags, packed in quality drums to protect the material. Small packs are available on request.

Can I order small quantities?

Yes — small packs are available on request alongside bulk and indent deals.

Do you provide a lab analysis report?

Yes. Our quality controllers provide a lab analysis report with each dispatch to assure quality.

Where does bulk material ship from?

Bulk orders are supplied from Kandla, Mundra, Hazira, JNPT and Aegis ports. Warehouse stock is held in Bhiwandi.

Can you source a chemical that is not on your list?

Very likely. We import and source on request through our network of leading manufacturers — send us the name and grade.
